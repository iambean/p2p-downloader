# Mora still-life artwork

`Resources/Artwork/WineStillLife.png` is a transparent decorative asset generated
with the built-in ImageGen tool from the approved Mora UI concept. It is bundled
locally, loaded once, excluded from accessibility, and does not intercept input.
No wine brand or third-party product label appears in the artwork.

Generation prompt:

> Extract and faithfully recreate only the wine still life from the upper-right
> of the approved UI mockup as a production decorative asset on a transparent
> background. No UI, text, window, background rectangle or ground plane. Arrange
> a slender dark wine bottle with burgundy capsule in the center, a clear
> thin-stem red wine glass in front left, and a narrow charcoal wine gift box with
> understated burgundy ribbon behind right. Add a short loose ribbon tail along
> the base. Keep all objects fully visible and grouped closely. Use refined,
> understated materials, narrow warm edge highlights, dark charcoal and muted
> burgundy, without labels. Preserve the reference's silhouette and low-key
> studio lighting. Use minimal transparent margins, a wider-than-tall centered
> composition and only a slight contact shadow fading to transparency. The asset
> will appear around 250 × 170 logical pixels over a flat dark header.

The UI itself remains native SwiftUI/AppKit; the asset is not a screenshot overlay.
